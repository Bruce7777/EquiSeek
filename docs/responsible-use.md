# Responsible use and regulatory boundaries

EquiSeek is a local-first investment-research and software-engineering project. It helps users organize public information, test explicit rules, compare scenarios, and preserve research evidence. It is not a broker, does not connect to brokerage accounts, does not place orders automatically, and does not promise returns.

## Interpreting outputs

Outputs may combine historical market data, deterministic calculations, configured rules, and model-assisted explanations. They can be incomplete, stale, or wrong. Before acting, verify the original source, timestamp, assumptions, calculation method, and invalidation conditions. Backtests and simulated results do not establish future performance.

## Data, models, and local information

Users and operators are responsible for complying with market-data licenses, model-provider terms, dependency licenses, and applicable privacy obligations. Keep API credentials outside the repository. Portfolio quantity, cost, dates, notes, and generated workspaces may be sensitive personal information; prefer local storage, least-privilege access, encryption, and deliberate retention policies.

## Public or commercial operation

An open-source license and local deployment do not create a regulatory exemption. Before offering public, paid, or personalized services involving specific securities or trading timing, obtain professional advice for the intended jurisdiction and assess at least:

- required securities-investment-advisory or related licenses and personnel qualifications;
- client suitability, risk disclosure, contracts, complaint handling, and audit records;
- market-data, news, research-content, and model-output rights;
- privacy, cybersecurity, cross-border data, and generative-AI governance duties;
- marketing claims, performance presentation, conflicts of interest, and human oversight.

This document describes product boundaries, not legal, tax, or investment advice. The person deploying or using EquiSeek remains responsible for decisions and compliance.
