WHAT EQUISEEK DOES

Not a general chatbot.
A research AI assistant.

Natural language is the entry point. Skills, tools, task planning, and sustained execution keep the work focused on screening, research, observation, and review.

01
⌁

Research AI assistant

Start research in natural language and combine Skills, workspace tools, and model capabilities. Dynamic conversation only requires your own DeepSeek API key.

  • Sustained, multi-step execution
  • Goals, Plans, and run traces
02
⌗

Stock research

Calculate MA, MACD, KDJ, RSI, ATR, BOLL, and WR locally, with multi-timeframe triggers, invalidation conditions, and risk boundaries.

  • A-shares and selected domestic indices and funds
  • BaoStock / optional Tushare
03
◎

Decision journal

Keep research conclusions on your computer and follow them with same-source closing prices. Outcomes are labeled as hypothetical reviews, never real trading performance.

  • Revisit conclusions at any time
  • Track price changes from the same source
04
◇

Portfolio, watchlists & Skills

Maintain local positions, watchlists, and candidates. Inspect, enable, or extend research Skills while preserving clear permission boundaries.

  • Data stored locally by default
  • Inspectable rules and permissions

DECISION JOURNAL

A conclusion is not the finish line.
Review is.

EquiSeek preserves the data cutoff, rule-based conclusion, risk score, and invalidation conditions in one evidence chain. Later, you see more than price movement—you can judge whether the original reasoning held up.

ConsistentResearch and follow-up use the same market-data source whenever possible
TransparentCalculation basis, time range, and data freshness remain visible
DisciplinedWaiting is a valid conclusion; uncertainty is never dressed up as certainty

KNOW THE BOUNDARIES

Research tools should state
what they cannot do.

01

Not a broker; no automated trading

EquiSeek does not connect to brokers, accept trade instructions, or execute orders for users.

02

No promise of returns

Rule-based and model-generated content is for research only. It can be wrong, stale, or unsuitable for your risk tolerance.

03

Your own DeepSeek key is enough

No maintainer key is bundled. Without a key, the app runs only clearly labeled deterministic local rules; Tushare and Tavily remain optional.

04

Market coverage is currently limited

Current coverage centers on A-shares and selected domestic indices and funds. US, Hong Kong, and other global assets are not yet integrated.
